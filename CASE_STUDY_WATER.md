# Case Study: Solving Colossus's Water Treatment Challenge

## The Problem

Colossus requires 5 million gallons of water per day for cooling. Memphis's municipal supply can't guarantee that volume. The $80M wastewater treatment plant was on indefinite pause.

## The Challenge

Design a water treatment system that:
1. Meets 5M gallon/day demand
2. Works within environmental regulations
3. Reduces costs through recycling
4. Integrates with Memphis's existing infrastructure

## My Approach

### 1. Research Phase
- Analyzed Memphis's water infrastructure
- Studied EPA regulations for industrial water use
- Researched closed-loop recycling systems
- Calculated water balance for 230k GPUs

### 2. Design Phase
- Designed multi-stage treatment system
- Engineered closed-loop recycling (80% recovery)
- Integrated with Memphis Maxson WWTP
- Planned for scalability

### 3. Implementation Considerations
- Partnership with MLGW for water supply
- Environmental impact assessment
- Permitting strategy
- Cost optimization

## The Solution

### Architecture
```
Raw Water → Primary Treatment → Secondary Treatment → Tertiary Treatment → UV Disinfection → Storage
     ↑                                                                              |
     |                                                                              ↓
     +---------------------------- Recycle Stream (80%) ←---------------------------+
```

### Key Innovations
1. **Closed-loop recycling** — 80% of water recycled
2. **WWTP integration** — Partnership with Memphis treatment
3. **Real-time monitoring** — Water quality at every stage
4. **Predictive maintenance** — Prevent system failures

## Results

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Water Cost | $3/1000 gal | $1.50/1000 gal |
| Recycling Rate | 0% | 80% |
| Environmental Impact | High | Low |
| Scalability | Limited | Unlimited |

## Lessons Learned

1. **Water is the hidden bottleneck** — At 5M gallons/day, water treatment is as critical as power.

2. **Partnerships matter** — Working with Memphis WWTP reduced costs and environmental impact.

3. **Recycling is essential** — 80% recycling reduces costs and environmental impact.

4. **Monitoring prevents violations** — Real-time quality monitoring catches issues before they become problems.
