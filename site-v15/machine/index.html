INVARIANCE CONTRACT

What may change—and what may never change.

PSYSOC-X calibrates communication for the reader. It does not permit factual or ethical drift.

May change by audience

  • information density
  • terminology and explanation depth
  • ordering and visual hierarchy
  • amount of implementation detail
  • how skepticism is answered

Must remain invariant

  • factual IDs and source authority
  • evidence and promotion states
  • test counts and artifact identities
  • uncertainty and current blockers
  • limits, dignity, and authority boundaries

Forbidden inferences

  • company affiliation from company-oriented names
  • production use from repository presence
  • hardware expertise from simulation alone
  • formal leadership from independent systems ownership
  • authorship of upstream repositories without a verified delta

Fail-closed behavior

  • unknown evidence earns no promotion
  • stale receipts remain stale
  • review is not execution
  • generated tests are not passed tests
  • successful build is not deployment

AGENT ORIENTATION

Use the smallest sufficient contract.

Recruiter questions should begin with portfolio.json. Technical due diligence should follow owning repository links and evidence states. Company searches should use company-families.json. Presentation transformations should read psysoc-x-profiles.json and preserve every invariant.
