Reliability & operations · Sigma Glue Orchestration

Recover an uncertain write before deciding to retry.

The problem

A provider may apply a write and lose the success response. Retrying blindly can repeat a side effect.

What I built

Use provider-aware recovery, restart-readable evidence, and replay guards to reconcile an ambiguous execution.

The operating decisions

Treat an unknown outcome as a decision to investigate. Read provider state, preserve the recovery trail, and block a repeat action once completion is established.

Why it matters

Shows the judgment behind dependable automation: knowing when to act, when to inspect, and when to stop.
