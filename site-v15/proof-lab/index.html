Why this exists

Many portfolio pages make their readers trust large numbers and dense technical language. This product offers a simpler experience: choose a claim, execute a real provider check, inspect a bounded result.

The live inspection is a service/API integration with failure handling and a script-free, accessible HTML interface. The GitHub sources remain the authorities for recorded execution and source identity.

What this does not prove

A matching commit proves the commit exists. It does not prove customer adoption, production traffic, latency, cost, or business impact. A successful workflow receipt proves that one recorded run, not every possible workload.

Provider checks are sampled on demand and cached briefly when successful. If an upstream service is unavailable or rate-limited, the result stays explicitly unresolved.
