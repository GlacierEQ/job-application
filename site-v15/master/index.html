REPEATED ENGINEERING PATTERNS

Fail-closed execution envelopes

Bounded state transitions

proposed state → validate → admit | defer | confirm | deny | invalidate → reason

Idempotent recovery + provenance

Truth-preserving public projections

Reversible verified change

Preserve known-good state, gate mutation, verify the changed state, and restore when post-change checks fail.
