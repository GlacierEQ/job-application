# xAI — Statement of Exceptional Work

## Draft

I build systems for AI operations at scale.

My recent work centers on a Colossus-inspired infrastructure portfolio covering cooling, energy, security, servers, deployment, memory, and model-to-agent coordination. Rather than treating these as isolated projects, I organized them as one operating system for AI infrastructure: GitHub as source of truth, Supabase for identity and relational state, Vercel for deployment, Supermemory for persistent context, and Aspen Grove / M2A for auditable model-to-agent routing.

The strongest example is the `xai-colossus-cooling` work. It evolved from a thermal-control concept into a broader runtime layer with responder registries, weighted routing, degraded-node suppression, route health, audit events, and dashboard visibility. The larger Colossus portfolio extends that systems approach across energy, water, security, servers, microcode, and build planning.

I work best when the problem is under-specified, operationally important, and moving quickly. I am comfortable moving between architecture, code, deployment, automation, and documentation. I do not wait for a perfect task definition; I build the map, identify the bottleneck, and produce a working system that can be inspected and improved.

What I would bring to xAI:

- independent technical execution
- systems-level thinking across software and physical infrastructure
- AI memory and orchestration architecture
- connector and MCP integration
- deployment and CI automation
- strong written technical communication
- willingness to work directly on difficult operational problems

Selected proof-of-work:

- `GlacierEQ/xai-colossus-cooling`
- `GlacierEQ/xai-colossus-energy`

I am looking for a role where unusual breadth is useful: AI infrastructure, runtime systems, observability, deployment, data-center operations software, or model/tool orchestration. I would welcome the opportunity to walk through the architecture, the engineering tradeoffs, and what I would improve next.
