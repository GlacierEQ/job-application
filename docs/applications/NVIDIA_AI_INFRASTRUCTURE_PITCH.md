# NVIDIA — AI Infrastructure Pitch

## Draft

I build the connective systems around AI infrastructure: orchestration, operational memory, deployment automation, health visibility, and source-of-truth control planes.

My recent portfolio began with Colossus-inspired infrastructure work and expanded into a wider architecture spanning cooling, energy, security, servers, deployment, memory, and model-to-agent routing. The goal was not to imitate hardware design. The goal was to understand and build the software systems required to operate complex AI infrastructure reliably.

Selected proof-of-work:

- `GlacierEQ/xai-colossus-energy`

I am especially interested in roles at the intersection of AI infrastructure, GPU/HPC systems, data-center software, networking, platform engineering, and technical solutions architecture.
