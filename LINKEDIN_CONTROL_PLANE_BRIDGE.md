## Separation rule

The public hiring profile is a projection of verified hiring facts. It must not import accusations, legal hypotheses, private evidence, or unsupported claims from the case layer.

The case layer may ingest profile history, recruiter activity, application results, support/security events, and employer decisions as source-linked events.
